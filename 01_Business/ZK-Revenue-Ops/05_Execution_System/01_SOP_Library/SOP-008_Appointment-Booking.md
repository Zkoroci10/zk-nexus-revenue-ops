# SOP-008 — Appointment Booking

## Goal
Mengunci slot temujanji viewing fizikal, menghasilkan *Buyer Dossier*, dan melaksana Protokol Anti-Ghosting T-24j & T-2j.

* **Layer 2 (Tool Guide)**: Buat kad temujanji dalam Notion Calendar & Waze dispatch.
* **Layer 3 (Automation)**: Bot WhatsApp hantar peringatan T-24j & T-2j automatik.
