# ZK-AUT-011 — Automation Backlog & Phased Roadmap

> **ZK-AUT-011 | Senarai Tugasan Automasi & Pelan Pelaksanaan 3-Fasa (Automation Backlog)**

---

## Phased Implementation Sequence Roadmap

```text
[Step 1: Document Candidates] ──> [Step 2: Automate Simple (First 30 Days)] ──> [Step 3: AI Layer (After 3+ Clients)]
```

* **Step 1 — Document Automation Candidate**: Menyenaraikan proses manual, kekerapan, masa digunakan, dan potensi automasi.
* **Step 2 — Automate Simple Candidate (First 30 Days)**:
  * Automasi pembersihan format nombor telefon.
  * Automasi peringatan tugasan susulan operator.
  * Automasi penjanaan & hantaran Laporan Audit Mingguan (Isnin 9am).
* **Step 3 — Build AI Layer (After 3+ Clients & Stable SOP)**: Integrasi modul AI Triage, AI Follow-up Suggestion, & AI QA Checker selepas aliran kerja dan SOP tidak lagi berubah setiap minggu.
