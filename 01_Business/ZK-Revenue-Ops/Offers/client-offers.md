# ZK Revenue Ops Client Offers

This document details the active business propositions (Offers) presented to potential clients.

---

## 1. Offer: 30-Day REN SDR Starter Pilot

### 1.1 Value Proposition
"Stop losing property commissions to neglected leads. We qualify and revive your cold leads, pre-screen their DSR, and schedule viewing appointments—all for the price of a dinner (RM0-200)."

### 1.2 Target Audience
Individual property agents (REN) in Malaysia who have:
- 10 to 50 dormant leads from past campaigns.
- No CRM system or structured spreadsheet.
- High focus on closing deals but no time for constant follow-up.

### 1.3 Offer Structure
- **Pilot Price:** RM 199 (Introductory Rate).
- **Service Period:** 30 Days.
- **Lead Limit:** Up to 30 active or cold leads.
- **Deliverables:**
  - One-time cleanup of raw lead list.
  - WhatsApp qualification script execution (up to 3 follow-up attempts).
  - Pre-screening of mortgage capacity (DSR Calculator).
  - Viewing appointment booking.

## 2. Offer: Established SDR Campaign Package

### 2.1 Value Proposition
"A dedicated sales development team in your pocket. We run daily lead qualification, pipeline cleanups, automated broadcasts, and ghost lead scanners to maximize your project conversion."

### 2.2 Target Audience
Successful RENs, team leaders, or agency partners who run high-budget Facebook/TikTok lead ads ($\ge RM 1,000/mo$).

### 2.3 Offer Structure
- **Price Tiers:**
  - **Single Agent:** RM 2,500/month.
  - **Property Team (Up to 3 agents):** RM 4,500/month.
- **Service Period:** Month-to-month.
- **Lead Limit:** Up to 150 incoming leads/month.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Dedicated virtual assistant (VA SDR) active Monday-Friday.
  - Live Internal CRM management (War Room panel).
  - Automated WhatsApp broadcast setup.
  - Daily "Ghost Revival" scanner sweeps.
  - Pre-approvals and DSR checking.
