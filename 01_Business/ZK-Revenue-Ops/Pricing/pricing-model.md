# ZK Revenue Ops Pricing Model

This document outlines the pricing model and packages for ZK Revenue Ops (BUS-001).

---

## 1. Package Matrix

| Package Name | Target Client | Price | Duration | Lead Limit | Core Features |
|--------------|---------------|-------|----------|------------|---------------|
| **REN Starter Pilot** | Emerging RENs looking for proof of concept | RM 0 - RM 200 | 30 Days | Up to 30 Leads | WhatsApp outreach, basic DSR screening, 3 follow-up touches, client dashboard integration |
| **Established SDR Growth** | Active RENs / Teams with regular ad campaigns | RM 2,500 - RM 4,500 | Month-to-Month | Up to 150 Leads | Full CRM setup, daily ghost scanning & revival, unlimited touches (up to T7), priority appointment booking |

---

## 2. Package Inclusions & Definitions

### 2.1 REN Starter Pilot (Trial Pack)
* **Goal:** Demonstrate immediate pipeline value, organize messy leads, and qualify active prospects.
* **Pricing Rationale:** Low-barrier entry (RM0-200) to build case studies and establish confidence.
* **Deliverables:**
  - Standard spreadsheet CRM setup (War Room sheet).
  - Manual WhatsApp follow-up using standard templates.
  - Appointment setting and handover.

### 2.2 Established SDR Growth Package
* **Goal:** Serve as a dedicated outbound development team for high-volume property agents.
* **Pricing Rationale:** Premium monthly rate (RM2,500 - RM4,500) compensating dedicated VA hours and advanced automation tools.
* **Deliverables:**
  - Custom internal CRM panel with client token portal.
  - Automated WhatsApp broadcast generator.
  - Daily "Ghost Scanner" sweeps to revive cold leads.
  - DSR screening for mortgage pre-qualification.

---

## 3. Terms of Payment
- **Pilot Pack:** Upfront payment.
- **Growth Pack:** Monthly retainer due on the 1st of each billing month. 
- **Refund Policy:** No refunds for completed monthly service cycles.
