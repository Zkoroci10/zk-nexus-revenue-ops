# Statement of Work (SOW) — SDR Pilot Campaign

This Statement of Work (SOW) is entered into by and between **ZK Revenue Operations** (Provider) and **Ahmad PJ** representing **IQI Realty** (Client) on **2026-07-18**.

---

## 1. Scope of Services
Provider will perform the following sales development and lead qualification services for the Client:

1. **Database Setup:** Import up to 30 raw prospects into the ZK Revenue Ops WebApp.
2. **Outreach Execution:** Contact leads via WhatsApp using the standardized T1-T7 sequence outlined in **SOP-003**.
3. **Mortgage Qualification:** Screen leads for Debt Service Ratio (DSR) based on the client's property price (Petaling Jaya projects).
4. **Appointment Setting:** Schedule property viewings directly with the Client for leads verified with "Strong" or "Borderline" DSR.

---

## 2. Deliverables & Schedule
- **Onboarding:** Client supplies lead CSV within 2 days of signing.
- **Delivery Period:** 30 calendar days starting from the receipt of the CSV lead dump.
- **Reporting:** Weekly lead pipeline summary report sent to Client via WhatsApp.

---

## 3. Fees & Payment
- **Service Fee:** Flat fee of **RM 199** for the 30-day pilot.
- **Payment Schedule:** 100% setup fee due upfront before onboarding begins.
- **Method:** Bank transfer to ZK Revenue Operations account.
