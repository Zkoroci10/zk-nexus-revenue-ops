## 1. Executive Summary
As a property agent, your time is best spent meeting clients and closing sales. However, up to 70% of potential commissions are lost due to slow lead response or lack of structured follow-up. 

This proposal outlines a 30-day **Sales Development Representative (SDR) Pilot** where ZK Revenue Ops qualifies, screens, and organizes your property leads so you only attend high-intent viewing appointments.

---

## 2. Inclusions & Deliverables

We take over your pipeline of up to **30** leads and execute the following:

- **Pipeline Cleanup:** Import and organize raw leads into a clean, prioritized spreadsheet.
- **WhatsApp Qualification Campaign:** Up to 7 touches per lead using real-time scripts.
- **DSR Eligibility Screening:** Check leads' mortgage capacity (Monthly Income vs. Commitments) before arranging viewings.
- **Appointment Scheduling:** Hand over qualified leads directly into your calendar for viewing slots.
- **Real-Time Client Dashboard:** 24/7 access to your lead status panel.
