# IDX-016: Listing-Based Pitch Matrix (ABM Strategy)

**Purpose:** This matrix guides the ZK Revenue Ops SDR/VA on how to personalize the cold outreach (Touch 1) based on the specific type of listing that a target agent (REN) is currently advertising. This increases response rates by showing deep context.

---

## 1. Segmentation Matrix

| Segment | Target Listing Types | Core Pain Point | WhatsApp Outreach Hook (Malay/Rojak) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **A. Luxury Subsale** | Condos/Landed > RM 1 Million (e.g. Mont Kiara, Bangsar, KLCC, Desa ParkCity) | **DSR Failure:** High-net-worth buyers with complex incomes failing bank loan checks. | Focus on **Debt Service Ratio (DSR) & Mortgage qualification check** before arranging viewing. |
| **B. Mass New Launch** | Developer project launches < RM 600k (e.g. Setapak, Bangi, Cybersouth, Elmina) | **Lead Overload:** Too many low-quality FB Ads leads; cannot contact all within 5 minutes. | Focus on **Speed-to-Lead Automation** & fast WhatsApp nurturing. |
| **C. Dormant/Old Landed** | Subsale Terrace/Semi-D (e.g. Shah Alam, Cheras, Kajang, PJ) | **Stale Database:** Old leads from 3-6 months ago sitting dormant in their phone/Excel. | Focus on **Lead Revival Campaign** (extracting gold from old databases). |

---

## 2. Segment-Specific WhatsApp Outreach Templates (T1)

### 2.1 Segment A: Luxury Subsale Ejen (DSR Qualification Focus)
* **VA Trigger:** Agent has active listings of condos or bungalows in high-end areas.
* **T1 WhatsApp Script:**
  > *"Hi [Nama Ejen], saya nampak listing unit [Nama Condo / Garden] anda di [Kawasan, e.g. Mont Kiara] dekat iProperty. Cantik unit tu.*
  >
  > *Saja nak tanya, untuk unit premium macam ni, selalu tak anda jumpa buyer yang minat tapi sangkut DSR bank sebab komitmen luar tinggi?*
  >
  > *SDR team kami di ZK Revenue Ops ada bantu ejen hartanah tapis DSR gaji & komitmen buyer awal-awal sebelum set viewing slot. Minat nak tengok contoh portal DSR helper kami secara free?"*

---

### 2.2 Segment B: Mass New Launch Ejen (Speed-to-Lead Focus)
* **VA Trigger:** Agent posts high-volume Facebook ads or is part of a marketing team for a new project launch.
* **T1 WhatsApp Script:**
  > *"Hi [Nama Ejen], nampak anda aktif promosikan projek baru [Nama Projek] kat FB Ads. Cantik sales package projek tu.*
  >
  > *Bila lead masuk mencurah-curah dari ads, sempat tak anda follow-up semua dalam masa 5 minit sebelum lead pergi tanya ejen lain?*
  >
  > *Team kami bantu ejen uruskan Speed-to-Lead respon pantas 24/7 dan tapis kelayakan mereka secara automatik. Boleh saya share case study ringkas macam mana kami bantu ejen dapatkan confirmed viewing slot?"*

---

### 2.3 Segment C: Dormant Subsale Landed Ejen (Lead Revival Focus)
* **VA Trigger:** Agent lists subsale terrace houses or lists properties in older residential zones.
* **T1 WhatsApp Script:**
  > *"Hi [Nama Ejen], saya nampak listing rumah teres di [Kawasan, e.g. Shah Alam Seksyen 7] yang anda iklankan.*
  >
  > *Kebanyakan ejen ada banyak lead lama dari iklan bulan lepas yang tersimpan dalam telefon begitu sahaja. Sayang kalau dibiar sejuk.*
  >
  > *Kami boleh tolong run kempen Lead Revival (WhatsApp follow-up bertingkat) ke atas database lama anda untuk tapis semula buyer yang aktif berminat. Anda hanya bayar RM199 flat rate. Boleh saya hantar draf proposal pilot kami?"*

---

## 3. VA Workflow Checklist
1. **Prospect Check:** Open the target agent's profile on iProperty/PropertyGuru/Facebook.
2. **Determine Segment:** Classify the listings into Segment A (Luxury), Segment B (New Launch), or Segment C (Dormant Landed).
3. **Copy & Customize:** Use the corresponding script and fill in:
   - `[Nama Ejen]`
   - `[Nama Condo / Projek / Kawasan]`
4. **Send & Log:** Send via WhatsApp and record the outreach category in the `Activity Log` sheet tab.
