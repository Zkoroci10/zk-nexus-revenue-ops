# ZK Revenue Ops — Service Catalog

> **ZK-OPS-001 | Katalog Perkhidmatan & Penawaran SDR ZK Revenue Ops**

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

**ZK Revenue Ops** provides AI-powered, virtual Sales Development Representative (SDR) operations tailored exclusively for **Real Estate Negotiators (REN)** in Malaysia. 

Most active property agents waste up to 70% of their operational time dealing with cold leads, uncontactable inquiries, and buyers who fail basic bank loan Debt Service Ratio (DSR) checks. ZK Revenue Ops automates the entire lead triage pipeline—engaging leads within minutes via WhatsApp, verifying financial eligibility, handling objections, and booking qualified viewing appointments directly into the agent’s calendar.

---

## 2. Governance & Exclusive Seat Cap (ZK-GOV-001)

Per **ZK-GOV-001 (Master Business Rules)**, ZK Revenue Ops operates under strict scarcity and exclusivity:

* **30 Exclusive REN Seats (`SEAT-001` to `SEAT-030`)**: Service is strictly capped at **30 active Solo REN seats nationwide**.
* **Zero Database Collision Guarantee**: No two subscribed RENs are assigned the same property project or sub-district zone. Leads and property listing data are strictly segregated.
* **Waitlist Protocol**: Upon reaching 30 seats, new applicant RENs are placed on an automated Waitlist until an existing seat is revoked or retired.

## 4. Detailed Pricing Models & Payment Terms

1. **Pilot Tier (RM 199 One-Off)**:
   - 100% upfront payment upon SOW signing.
   - Valid for 30 consecutive calendar days or up to 30 processed leads (whichever comes first).
   - Non-refundable setup fee designed for model verification.

2. **Core Retainer Tier (RM 1,999/month)**:
   - Monthly advance billing due on the 1st of each calendar month.
   - 7-day grace period. Failure to settle on or before day 7 triggers automatic seat revocation (**BR-004.2**).
   - Includes 150 lead processing capacity; overage billed at RM 15 per additional qualified lead.

3. **Growth Retainer Tier (RM 3,999/month)**:
   - Monthly advance billing with dedicated AI SDR instance and custom n8n workflow integrations.
   - Includes 400 lead processing capacity; overage billed at RM 12 per additional qualified lead.
