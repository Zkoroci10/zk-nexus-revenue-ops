# ZK Revenue Ops Outreach SOP (SOP-003)

**Purpose:** Standard operating procedure for the virtual assistant (VA SDR) executing follow-ups on property leads.

---

## 1. Timeline of Touches (Follow-Up Sequence)

Every property lead must receive a structured contact sequence spanning 20+ days until they respond or are closed.

```text
  T1 (Day 1)      T2 (Day 2)      T3 (Day 4)      T4 (Day 7)      T5 (Day 10)     T6 (Day 14)     T7 (Day 20+)
  Intro           Alternate       Market News     DSR Check       Ghost Warning   Final Call      Revival Pitch
```

| Touch | Timing | Channel | In Malay / Manglish |
|-------|--------|---------|---------------------|
| **T1** | Day 1 (Intake) | WhatsApp | *"Hi [Name], saya dari ZK Revenue Ops mewakili [REN Name]. Nampak you berminat dengan [Property Name]. Unit still available, you nak check viewing slot minggu ni?"* |
| **T2** | Day 2 | WhatsApp | *"Hi [Name], just checking in. Selain [Property Name], kita ada match unit baru dekat area [Area]. Still looking or just explore?"* |
| **T3** | Day 4 | WhatsApp | *"Yo [Name]! Nak share sikit market update area [Area] - prices are going up soon. Just want to make sure you get early bird info."* |
| **T4** | Day 7 | WhatsApp | *"Hi [Name], sebelum set viewing slot, bank require us to run basic pre-check. Monthly income RM [X] stable? Sedia saya tolong kirakan DSR?"* |
| **T5** | Day 10 | WhatsApp | *"Hi [Name], busy lately? Kalau you not active, I close file dulu ya. Let me know if still interested."* |
| **T6** | Day 14 | WhatsApp | *"Final follow-up [Name]. I close file ini dulu ya. Any questions later, you can directly contact [REN Name]. Thanks!"* (Set status to Ghost). |
| **T7** | Day 20+ | WhatsApp | *(Used for Ghost revival)* *"Yo [Name]! Owner matching unit baru hijaukan harga nak letgo cepat. Still looking or not?"* |

---

## 2. Operational Rules

1. **Primary Channel:** Always use **WhatsApp** first. Only call or email if explicitly requested by the lead or if the phone number is invalid on WhatsApp.
2. **Database Logging:** Update the **War Room** sheet immediately after every touch:
   - Increment the Touch Count column (+1).
   - Update Last Contact Date.
   - Update next follow-up action.
3. **Tone of Voice:** Maintain a friendly, helpful, and professional tone. Use natural Manglish or Standard Malay as appropriate for the lead's initial communication style.
4. **Handover Protocol:** When a lead confirms a viewing slot (Appointment status):
   - Notify the property agent (REN) via WhatsApp immediately.
   - Transfer all DSR notes and client interest details to the agent.
   - Update status to **Appointment** in the CRM.
