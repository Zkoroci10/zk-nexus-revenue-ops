# ZK Revenue Ops — Malaysia 6 Macro Zones & Micro-Territory Exclusivity Matrix

## Executive Overview

To prevent lead cannibalization and ad targeting overlap between agents, ZK Revenue Ops caps nationwide agent onboarding at **30 RENs maximum**, organized into **6 Macro Zones** with strict **Micro-Submarket Territory Protection** (1 REN per distinct Sub-market/Parliamentary District).

## 2. Strategic Benefits of Micro-Territory Protection

1. **Zero Ad Targeting Overlap**: When REN A runs Meta/Google Ads targeting "Setia Alam", REN B in Shah Alam Section 7 will NOT compete for the same audience.
2. **Absolute Territory Exclusivity**: RENs pay for the service knowing they OWN the ZK Revenue Ops infrastructure for their specific sub-market.
3. **High Ticket Conversion**: RENs feel safe sharing their lead sources without fear of internal poaching.
