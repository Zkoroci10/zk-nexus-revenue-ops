# ZK Revenue Ops — Malaysia 6-Zone REN Territory Matrix

## Executive Overview

To maximize REN productivity and maintain single-tenant exclusivity, ZK Revenue Ops caps nationwide agent onboarding at **30 RENs maximum**, organized into **6 High-Volume Property Zones** with exactly **5 RENs per Zone**.

---

---

## 2. Strategic Rationale for 5 RENs / Zone Allocation

1. **Exclusivity Value Proposition**: RENs gain higher value knowing they are 1 of only 5 agents backed by ZK Revenue Ops in their entire territory.
2. **Lead Density**: Each REN receives a clean stream of ~5,000 legacy/cold leads + 100 fresh qualified buyer prospects without internal cannibalization.
3. **Target MRR Impact**:
   - 30 RENs × RM 500 (Starter Tier) = **RM 15,000 / month**
   - 30 RENs × RM 1,500 (Pro Tier) = **RM 45,000 / month**
   - Goal (RM 10k MRR) achieved at just **20 active RENs @ RM500/mo** or **7 active RENs @ RM1500/mo**.
