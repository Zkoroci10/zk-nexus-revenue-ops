## What This Is

ZK Nexus is not a company operating system, not a holding company, and not an ERP. It is a shared workspace where humans and AI systems collaborate through the same files and folders.

**The folder structure IS the communication protocol.**

This repository contains:
- Business operations for ZK Revenue Ops, DAE Ecosystem, and Future Ventures
- Active projects and deliverables
- Reusable knowledge, SOPs, and frameworks
- AI worker profiles, prompts, and role definitions
- Live automation, scripts, and system configs
- Shared media and brand assets
- Reusable templates
- Immutable logs of all decisions and changes
- Archived historical records

---

## How AI Collaborates Here

1. **AI reads first.** Every AI entering this workspace reads `00_Command Center/AI-START-HERE.md` first.
2. **AI stages work.** All new operational content is created in `02_Projects/` first, then reviewed before moving to live modules.
3. **AI logs everything.** Significant actions are recorded in `08_Logs/`.
4. **AI never edits Active files directly.** New versions are created as Draft and promoted through Review.
5. **AI hands over cleanly.** Cross-AI worker transfers use standardized handover notes.
