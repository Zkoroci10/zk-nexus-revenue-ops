## 1. Executive Summary

As a Real Estate Negotiator (REN), your most valuable asset is your time spent conducting viewings and closing sales. However, up to **70% of potential commissions are lost** due to slow lead response times, lack of structured follow-up, and taking unqualified buyers to physical viewings who ultimately fail bank Debt Service Ratio (DSR) loan screening.

This proposal outlines a 30-day **Sales Development Representative (SDR) Pilot Campaign** where ZK Revenue Ops deploys an AI-powered virtual SDR triage engine to clean, qualify, DSR-screen, and organize your lead pipeline—delivering pre-approved buyer appointments directly into your calendar.

---

## 2. Scope of Inclusions & Key Deliverables

During the 30-day pilot, ZK Revenue Ops processes up to **30 Raw Buyer Leads** provided by the client or generated via active campaign ads:

| Deliverable | Description & SLA Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pipeline Triage & Cleaning** | Import, format, and deduplicate up to 30 raw leads into an organized database. |
| **Speed-to-Lead Triage** | Automated initial WhatsApp engagement triggered within **< 5 minutes** (`WA-01`). |
| **DSR Loan Pre-Screening** | Verify buyer Net Income vs Bank Debt Commitments (Target DSR $\le 60\%$). |
| **1-100 Point Lead Scoring** | Categorize leads into Tier 1 Hot (`ST-03`), Tier 2 Warm (`ST-04`), or Tier 3 Disqualified (`ST-05`). |
| **Calendar Booking Sync** | Inject qualified viewing slots directly into the agent’s Google Calendar. |
| **T-24h & T-2h Verification** | Anti-ghosting verification protocol to ensure high viewing show-up rate (`ST-08`). |
| **24/7 Client Reporting Portal** | Real-time Notion / Web App dashboard providing live pipeline metrics. |
