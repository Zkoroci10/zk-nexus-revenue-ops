# Project Charter — PRJ-003 ZK Revenue Ops Business Readiness

---

## 1. Project Metadata
* **Project ID:** PRJ-003
* **Project Name:** ZK Revenue Ops Business Readiness
* **Objective:** Enable ZK Revenue Ops (BUS-001) for market execution by creating core business enablement documents and standardized workspace templates.
* **Owner:** AI-002 ZK Operator
* **Sponsor:** Human Founder

---

## 2. Background & Rationale
Now that all data is migrated (PRJ-001) and the workspace is cleaned (PRJ-002), ZK Revenue Ops needs specific business parameters defined—Service Catalog, Pricing Model, Client Offers, Sales Processes, Client Folder Standards, and outreach assets. 

Completing these makes the business "Active" and ready for the sales engine stage (PRJ-004) and automation (PRJ-005).

---

## 3. Scope of Work

### In Scope:
1. **Service Catalog:** Document SDR service details.
2. **Pricing Model:** Define the pilot and campaign tiers.
3. **Offers:** Describe trial packages.
4. **Sales Process:** Map the pipeline and funnel stages.
5. **Client Folder Structure:** Define standard folder templates.
6. **Proposal Template:** Create a reusable proposal format in `07_Templates/`.
7. **Email Template:** Create cold and warm outreach emails.
8. **Outreach SOP:** Create step-by-step VA workflows for follow-ups (SOP-003).
