## 3. Triage Evaluation Rules
1. **Impact Score**: High (Direct Revenue/Value), Medium (Efficiency/Time Saving), Low (Nice to have).
2. **Feasibility**: Easy (< 1 day build), Medium (2-3 days build), Hard (> 1 week build).
3. **Promotion Rule**: Maximum 3 ideas per week promoted from `Approved` ➔ `Active PRJ`.
