# Decision Log: Mandatory Authentic Founder Photo for WhatsApp Branding

**Timestamp:** 2026-07-29 16:00:00 +08:00  
**Actor:** Human Founder (CEO Ariff)  
**Decision Type:** Brand & Identity Policy  
**Status:** Active & Implemented  

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
During initial design iterations for WhatsApp Business outreach assets, AI-generated avatars and corporate logo placeholders were evaluated. In B2B sales within the Malaysian real estate industry, anonymous or synthetic avatars trigger trust resistance among agency leaders.

## 2. Decision Summary
Mandate **100% authentic photograph of Founder Ariff** across all WhatsApp Business profiles, cover banners, and catalog branding materials. AI avatars or faceless logo placeholders are strictly prohibited for primary founder outreach channels.

## 3. Key Rationale & Rationale Chain
- **Trust & Authority**: Agency leaders and senior RENs respond significantly better to real human founders than generic corporate bots or AI avatars.
- **Relatability**: Personal branding builds long-term industry reputation and relationship-driven deal closing.
- **Legal & Ethic Hygiene**: Avoids deception claims or perceived synthetic impersonation.

## 4. Impacted Systems & Scope
- `WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_BRANDING_BLUEPRINT.md` catalog & profile design specs.
- Founder avatar image assets & cover banner design guidelines.
