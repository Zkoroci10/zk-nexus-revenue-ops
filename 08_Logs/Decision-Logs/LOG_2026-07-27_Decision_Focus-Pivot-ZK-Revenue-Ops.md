# Decision Log: Exclusive 100% Focus Pivot to ZK Revenue Ops Platform

**Timestamp:** 2026-07-27 10:00:00 +08:00  
**Actor:** Human Founder (CEO Ariff)  
**Decision Type:** Strategic / Portfolio Pivot  
**Status:** Active & Implemented  

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
Prior to this decision, internal execution capacity was fragmented across multiple exploratory ventures including DAE (Digital Asset Engine) and future venture research. This split attention reduced development velocity and delayed launch milestones for B2B real estate client acquisitions.

## 2. Decision Summary
Founder Ariff formally decided to pause all secondary venture development (including DAE and generic AI consulting) and dedicate **100% of internal execution capacity and AI automation pipeline** exclusively to the **ZK Revenue Ops B2B Real Estate Platform**.

## 3. Key Rationale & Rationale Chain
- **Market Alignment**: High immediate demand among Malaysian Real Estate Negotiators (RENs) for qualified leads and automated lead nurturing.
- **Resource Concentration**: Focusing on a single high-ticket productized service (RM500 setup promo / RM3,000 growth tier + deal commission override) accelerates cash flow and system validation.
- **Operational Clarity**: Eliminates multi-project context switching for both human leadership and AI workspace agents.

## 4. Impacted Systems & Scope
- **Paused**: DAE research, uncommitted software experiments.
- **Prioritized**: ZK Revenue Ops single-tenant portal, Auto-DSR engine, WhatsApp SDR automation, Notion CRM pipeline.
