# Decision Log: Dual-Stream Pricing Matrix v2.0 & Setup Cost Absorption

**Timestamp:** 2026-07-29 14:30:00 +08:00  
**Actor:** Human Founder (CEO Ariff)  
**Decision Type:** Commercial / Pricing Architecture  
**Status:** Active & Implemented  

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
Initial pricing models proposed high upfront setup capital fees (RM1,500 setup + recurring retainer), which created friction during initial cold outreach with Malaysian Real Estate Negotiators (RENs) who prefer low-barrier entry paired with performance upside.

## 2. Decision Summary
Adopt **Dual-Stream Pricing Matrix v2.0**:
1. **Upfront Subscription Tiers (Setup Cost Absorbed)**:
   - *Newcomer / Pilot Promo*: RM500 setup (discounted from RM1,500) for first 30 exclusive REN partners.
   - *Growth Tier*: RM3,000 setup for multi-agent/team setups.
2. **Performance Override Stream**:
   - 5% to 10% commission override on closed real estate transactions resulting from lead nurture pipeline.

## 3. Key Rationale & Rationale Chain
- **Lowering Friction**: Absorbing capital setup costs removes initial buyer hesitation during pilot outreach.
- **Upside Alignment**: Closed deal override aligns ZK Nexus revenue directly with client closing success.
- **Cap Protection**: Capping pilot participation at 30 exclusive RENs preserves scarcity and high service quality.

## 4. Impacted Systems & Scope
- Sales outreach blueprints (`PRICING_TIERS_AND_PILOT_PROGRAM_BLUEPRINT.md`).
- Client portal subscription onboarding & terms of service.
