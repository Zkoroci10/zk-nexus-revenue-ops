# Digital Products Business Context

## Project
Digital Products Division

## Purpose
Create scalable digital products that solve specific problems and can be distributed online.

## Main Goal
Transform knowledge, systems and frameworks into sellable assets.

## Product Types

- Ebooks
- Templates
- Dashboards
- Guides
- Prompt packs
- Toolkits

## Principle

Create valuable assets that can be improved, packaged and sold repeatedly.
