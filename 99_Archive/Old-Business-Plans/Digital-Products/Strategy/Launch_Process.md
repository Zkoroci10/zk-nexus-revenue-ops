# Digital Product Launch Process

## Phase 1: Research

- Find market problem
- Understand customer
- Analyze competitors

## Phase 2: Build

- Create first version
- Prepare documentation
- Create sales materials

## Phase 3: Launch

- Publish offer
- Collect feedback
- Improve conversion

## Phase 4: Scale

- Automate delivery
- Improve marketing
- Create new versions
