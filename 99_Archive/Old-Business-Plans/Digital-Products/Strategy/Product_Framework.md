# Digital Product Framework

## Step 1: Identify Problem

Find a specific painful problem faced by a target customer.

## Step 2: Build Solution

Create a simple asset that solves the problem.

## Step 3: Package

Improve:
- Design
- Documentation
- User experience
- Instructions

## Step 4: Validate

Test:
- Demand
- Feedback
- Sales

## Step 5: Improve

Collect data and upgrade the product.
