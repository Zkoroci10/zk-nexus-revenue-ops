# SOP-SDR-003: Multi-Touch Follow-Up Cadence

## 1. Purpose
Provides a structured follow-up schedule for un-contacted or warm property leads to maximize lead recovery without spamming.

## 2. Touchpoint Schedule

```text
[Day 0: Initial Touch] -> [Day 1: Value Add] -> [Day 3: Video Walkthrough] -> [Day 7: Re-engagement] -> [Day 14: Final Check]
```

| Touchpoint | Timing | Channel | Content Focus |
|------------|--------|---------|---------------|
| Touch 1 | Immediate (< 5 min) | WhatsApp | First Touch & Qualification Script (SOP-SDR-001) |
| Touch 2 | Day 1 (+24 hrs) | WhatsApp | Project Floor Plan PDF / Brochure Teaser |
| Touch 3 | Day 3 | WhatsApp | Short Video Tour / Show unit photo highlight |
| Touch 4 | Day 7 | WhatsApp | Special Promotion / Limited Unit Availability Alert |
| Touch 5 | Day 14 | WhatsApp | Break-up message / Opt-in confirmation |

---

## 3. Anti-Ghosting Protocol (Pre-Viewing)
For confirmed viewings, enforce the **T-24h** and **T-2h** confirmation check:

* **T-24 Hours**:  
  > *"Salam [Name]! Peringatan mesra untuk janji temu viewing [Project Name] esok jam [Time]. Jumpa di Sales Gallery ya. Landmark: [Location/Waze link]."*
* **T-2 Hours**:  
  > *"Salam [Name]! Saya dah on the way ke Sales Gallery [Project Name]. Sampai nanti maklumkan saya ya."*

If no reply by T-2h, tag as `High Ghost Risk` and notify REN to hold departure.
