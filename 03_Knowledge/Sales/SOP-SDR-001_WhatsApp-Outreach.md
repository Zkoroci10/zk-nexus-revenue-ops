# SOP-SDR-001: WhatsApp Cold & Warm Outreach Protocol

## 1. Purpose
Defines the standard operational procedure for initiating WhatsApp outreach to property buyers, REN prospects, and lead inquiries while maintaining high response rates and speed-to-lead.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all Console Operators (Human & AI SDR Workers) executing outreach for ZK Revenue Ops.

## 3. Outreach Rules
1. **Speed to Lead**: First touch must occur within **5 minutes** of lead ingestion.
2. **Language**: Use professional Manglish / Bahasa Pasar suitable for local Malaysian property buyers (friendly, direct, respectful).
3. **Primary Goal**: Confirm buyer interest, budget, and loan eligibility — DO NOT sell immediately on First Touch.

---

## 4. WhatsApp Script Blueprints

### Script A: New Buyer Ingestion (First Touch < 5 Mins)
> "Salam / Hi [Name]! Saya [Operator Name] wakil dari [REN Name / Agency]. Terima kasih sebab berminat dengan projek [Project Name]. 
> 
> Boleh saya tahu, encik/puan cari rumah ni untuk duduk sendiri atau pelaburan (investment) ya?"

### Script B: Qualification & DSR Check
> "Faham [Name]. Untuk projek [Project Name], ansuran bulanan anggaran sekitar RM [Monthly Installment]. 
> 
> Boleh saya bantu semak kelayakan awal? Gaji bersih bulanan (single/gabung nama) melepasi RM [Min Salary] tak?"

### Script C: Viewing Appointment Booking
> "Terbaik [Name]! Kelayakan awal dah nampak cantik. Slot viewing rumah contoh dibuka minggu ni:
> 
> Option 1: Sabtu (11:00 AM)  
> Option 2: Ahad (3:00 PM)  
> 
> Slot mana yang paling selesa untuk tuan/puan?"

## 5. Objection Handling Quick Guide
* **"Berapa harga penuh?"**  
  👉 *"Harga bermula dari RM [Price], tetapi promosi rebat [Rebate %] terhad minggu ni. Boleh saya hantar brochure ringkas ke WA ni?"*
* **"Boleh email brochure?"**  
  👉 *"Saya hantar terus dalam WhatsApp PDF supaya cepat dibuka. Selepas tengok 2 minit, maklumkan gambar susun atur mana encik paling suka ya."*
