# ⚖️ ZK EMPIRE LEGAL & COMPLIANCE SYSTEM v2.0

## 🎯 PURPOSE
Ensure 100% legal compliance, single-tenant data isolation, and data protection under Malaysian law for ZK Empire & ZK Revenue Ops.

## 🛡️ CORE LEGAL STANDARDS

### 1. PDPA Act 2010 Alignment (Act 709)
- All lead ingestion, DSR qualification, and prospect data processing are conducted in strict alignment with the **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA Act 709 Malaysia)**.
- *Legally Accurate Framing*: We state **"Built to PDPA 2010 Privacy Standards"** and **"Single-Tenant Data Isolation"** (avoiding false "Certified" claims).

### 2. Micro-Territory Exclusivity Protection
- 1 REN Client = 1 Exclusive Sub-Market / Parliamentary District (3km radius protection) to prevent ad overlap or lead collision between agents.

### 3. Data Sovereignty & Confidentiality
- Single-Tenant Encrypted Database Architecture (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit).
- 100% Lead Privacy Guarantee: Client prospect databases are never sold, shared, or co-mingled.
